Arabic Font Changer

Read Arabic in a beautiful font at a comfortable size on any website. Your choice is saved per site and re-applied automatically.

Turning it on

  1. Open the app and click “Open Safari Settings”.
  2. Tick “Arabic Font Changer”.
  3. Choose “Always Allow on Every Website”.
